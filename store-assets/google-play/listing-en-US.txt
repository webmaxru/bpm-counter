APP NAME

Realtime BPM Counter for DJs


SHORT DESCRIPTION

On-device BPM analysis, tap tempo, and metronome. Audio never leaves your phone.


FULL DESCRIPTION

Find a track's tempo without interrupting your workflow. BPM Techno is a free native BPM toolkit for DJs, producers, musicians, and anyone working with rhythm.

ANALYZE ON YOUR DEVICE

Record a short clip from your microphone or open an audio file. BPM Techno estimates beats per minute on this device with Realtime BPM Analyzer for Java/Kotlin.

Your recordings and imported files stay on the phone. BPM Techno does not upload audio for analysis.

CHOOSE THE RIGHT TOOL

- Record a few bars and analyze the clip.
- Open MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, or OGG files.
- Tap along manually when you can hear the pulse clearly.
- Use the built-in metronome to practice or check a tempo.

BUILT FOR REAL DJ TASKS

Use BPM Techno while preparing a set, checking an untagged track, practicing a transition, or confirming a tempo before you mix.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Microphone access is used only while you record. Deny it anytime in system settings. Optional share text is yours to send; the audio itself never leaves the device.

No account or subscription is required.

ACCURATE, WITH HONEST LIMITS

Automatic detection works best with a steady section containing clear kicks, snares, claps, or other rhythmic transients. Silence, speech, ambient intros, heavy syncopation, and noisy recordings can reduce accuracy.

Treat every automatic result as a strong starting point. Confirm the pulse by ear when it matters.
