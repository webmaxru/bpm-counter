SHORT DESCRIPTION

Measure live music tempo, tap a beat, analyze an audio URL, and convert BPM to milliseconds with private, offline-ready tools for DJs.


DESCRIPTION

Find a track's tempo without interrupting your workflow. BPM Techno is a free set of focused BPM tools for DJs, producers, musicians, and anyone working with rhythm.

Use the real-time counter when music is already playing through speakers. Allow microphone access, point your device toward a clear section of the track, and let the detector estimate its beats per minute. The microphone stream is analyzed on your device: BPM Techno does not upload or store your live audio.

Choose the tool that fits the moment:

- Detect BPM from music playing nearby.
- Tap along manually when you can hear the pulse clearly.
- Analyze a direct, browser-accessible MP3 or WAV URL.
- Convert BPM into whole, half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth, dotted, and triplet note durations.
- Compare half-time and double-time values when a result does not match the way you count the track.

BPM Techno is useful for preparing DJ sets, checking untagged tracks, practicing transitions, setting tempo-synced delays, and resolving common half-time or double-time readings. Practical guides explain how BPM detection works, how to improve accuracy, and how to apply tempo information while beatmatching.

The core counter and calculators are designed to remain useful offline after the app has loaded. An internet connection is still required for remote audio URLs and external resources.

Automatic BPM detection works best with a steady section containing clear rhythmic transients. Treat the result as a strong starting point, confirm it by ear, and check the related half-time or double-time value when needed.

Free to use. No account or subscription required.
