Find a track's tempo without interrupting your set prep. BPM Techno is a native iOS app with focused BPM tools for DJs, producers, musicians, and anyone working with rhythm.

RECORD AND ANALYZE

Point your device at speakers or monitors, capture a short clip, and let BPM Techno estimate beats per minute with Realtime BPM Analyzer for Swift. Analysis runs entirely on your device. Microphone audio is not uploaded or stored.

ANALYZE LOCAL FILES

Import MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, or CAF with the system document picker. Decode and detect tempo offline - useful when a track is untagged or you need a second opinion before you cue it.

TAP TEMPO AND METRONOME

Tap along when you can hear the pulse clearly. Share the result with the native Share Sheet. Use the built-in metronome with adjustable BPM and beats per bar when you need a click.

BUILT FOR REAL DJ TASKS

Use BPM Techno while preparing a set, checking an untagged track, practicing a transition, or resolving a common half-time or double-time reading. Candidate tempos help you compare related values when an automatic result needs confirmation by ear.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Recording, file analysis, tap tempo, and the metronome work on-device. No account or subscription is required. Files longer than 10 minutes are rejected so analysis stays practical on mobile hardware.

ACCURATE, WITH HONEST LIMITS

Automatic detection works best with a steady section containing clear kicks, snares, claps, or other rhythmic transients. Silence, speech, ambient intros, heavy syncopation, and distorted microphone input can reduce accuracy.

Treat every automatic result as a strong starting point. Confirm the pulse by ear and check related half-time or double-time candidates when needed.
