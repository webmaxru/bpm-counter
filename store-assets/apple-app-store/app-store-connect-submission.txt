APP REVIEW NOTES

BPM Techno does not require an account or login.

The microphone permission is used only to record a short clip for on-device
rhythm analysis with Realtime BPM Analyzer for Swift. Live microphone audio is
not uploaded or stored. After analysis, temporary recording files are deleted.

Local file analysis uses the system document picker. Supported formats include
MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, and CAF. Files longer than 10 minutes are rejected.

The app also includes tap tempo with haptic feedback, a native metronome, and
the system Share Sheet for BPM results.

There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, accounts, or third-party ad SDKs
in the native binary.

RELEASE PIPELINE

5. Dispatch .github/workflows/ios-release.yml with version 4.0.0
6. Wait for TestFlight processing, then add What to Test notes
7. Paste listing copy, upload screenshots/icon, complete App Privacy
