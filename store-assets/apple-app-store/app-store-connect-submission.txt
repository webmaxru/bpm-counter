APP REVIEW NOTES

BPM Techno does not require an account or login.

The microphone permission is used only to analyze rhythm locally on the
device. Live microphone audio is not uploaded or stored.

The Audio URL tool fetches a direct MP3 or WAV URL selected by the user and
analyzes it in memory. The remote host may receive normal network request
information.

The iOS app also supports local audio import through the system document
picker, haptic feedback for tap tempo, the native Share Sheet for BPM results,
and an audio session configured for measurement. Bundled app assets allow the
tools and guidance to open without loading a remote website shell.

The app contains clearly labeled Amazon affiliate recommendations that open
external merchant pages. It does not sell digital content or subscriptions.
